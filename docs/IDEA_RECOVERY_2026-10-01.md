## Cross-project doctrine

- Ideas are archived before they are executed.
- interest ≠ intent ≠ contract ≠ transaction ≠ commission ≠ cash.
- Claimed physical assets, commissions, volumes, partner relationships and regulatory permissions remain UNKNOWN until evidenced.
- No code is required for the first test when a human experiment can answer the question.
- No customer money is collected for a regulated/intermediation model before the role and contractual framework are clarified.
- A successful test does not authorize product expansion automatically; it creates evidence for the next decision.
- Refuted ideas remain archived with their reason for rejection.
