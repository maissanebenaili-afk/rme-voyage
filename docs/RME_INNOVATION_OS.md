# RME Innovation OS

## North Star
RME is deterministic decision infrastructure for uncertain journeys, opportunities and transactions.

OBSERVE -> NORMALIZE -> PROVE -> SCORE -> DECIDE -> ACT -> MEASURE -> LEARN

## Reusable primitives
- Evidence Ledger: source, timestamp, confidence and state for every material fact.
- Risk Graph: transport, hotel and multimodal edges as explicit nodes and edges.
- Opportunity Graph: grants, tenders, partners, affiliate offers and B2B leads share the evidence model.
- Decision Capsule: replayable explanation of why an action is allowed, rejected or uncertain.
- Revenue Ledger: clicks, bookings, confirmations and commissions remain separate states.
- Replay Engine: reproduce a decision from frozen input and engine/configuration versions.

## Invariants
1. Unknown is never safe.
2. Assertions never become evidence.
3. LLMs may explain or propose hypotheses; deterministic engines own safety-critical calculations.
4. Dynamic facts require freshness and provenance.
5. No feature enters production without a measurable outcome.
6. Commercial incentives never mutate risk facts.

## Innovation tracks
- Counterfactual Travel: mutate one declared route/hotel/edge variable and measure deltas.
- Decision Replay: reproduce historical decisions without silently reconciling version drift.
- Opportunity Radar: reuse evidence, risk and revenue primitives for funding, procurement and B2B opportunities.
- Friction Learning: compare predicted friction with observed outcomes without changing safety thresholds automatically.
- Revenue-aware Routing: optimize utility separately from commercial incentives.
