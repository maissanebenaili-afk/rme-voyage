## Rules

1. Blank is not zero.
2. Do not enter simulated values as observed.
3. Keep failures.
4. Record the first safe/useful action, not the longest answer.
5. Do not change scenario wording between modes.
6. Record exact participant effort where possible.
7. Any factual external claim used during a test must retain its source.
8. No booking, payment, affiliate conversion, or revenue may be represented as observed unless it actually occurred.

## Analysis after collection

Calculate by mode:

- median context repeated;
- median questions;
- median searches;
- median time-to-useful-action;
- actionability distribution;
- continuity score;
- change-detection score;
- uncertainty-failure rate;
- unnecessary-interruption rate;
- effort distribution.

Do not declare a moat from a single scenario or anecdote.
